## Full description

Dipsan Academy brings your mock exam platform to your phone.

Students can:
• Browse published papers for Physics, Chemistry, Maths, Biology and more
• Sit timed CBT-style exams with a question palette and autosave
• Review detailed solutions and download a scorecard

Teachers can:
• Create and publish exams with image-based questions
• Track submissions, violations, and analytics
• Share video solutions with the class

Built for serious practice — clean, focused, and ready whenever you are.
